# 🎬 Netflix Movies & TV Shows Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Netflix's content library, uncover viewing-content trends, and visualize the distribution of movies and TV shows across countries, genres, ratings, and release years.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores Netflix's Movies and TV Shows dataset to understand its content distribution and historical release trends. The dashboard transforms raw data into meaningful visual insights through interactive charts, KPI cards, and geographical visualizations.

It is designed to help users explore Netflix's content catalogue and understand patterns that can support data-driven content analysis and strategic planning.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the distribution of movies and TV shows on Netflix.
- Examine content ratings and genre/category popularity.
- Explore release-year trends and changes in content additions.
- Visualize the geographical distribution of Netflix titles.
- Present key metrics through an interactive, user-friendly dashboard.

## 📈 Key Insights

- **Total Titles:** 8,809
- **Movies:** 6,131 (69.6%)
- **TV Shows:** 2,678 (30.4%)
- **Total Directors:** 4,527
- **Total Listed Categories/Genres:** 515
- **Content Release-Year Range:** 1925–2021

### 🔍 Dashboard Analysis

**1. Content Distribution**
- Compares the number of movies and TV shows.
- Highlights the dominance of movies in the dataset.

**2. Ratings Analysis**
- Examines content ratings such as TV-MA, TV-14, TV-PG, R, and PG-13.
- Helps identify the distribution of content across rating categories.

**3. Genre and Category Analysis**
- Highlights categories such as Dramas, Documentaries, Stand-Up Comedy, and International content.
- Supports exploration of the types of content represented in the catalogue.

**4. Release-Year Trends**
- Visualizes the distribution of movies and TV shows by release year.
- Shows a notable increase in content released during the later years, particularly after 2010.

**5. Geographical Distribution**
- Uses a world map to visualize Netflix titles by country.
- Enables exploration of the catalogue's geographical spread.
